Payment Report

By clicking this option, users can download the Payment Report.

Required Details

On this page, users must fill in the required details to generate the payment report.
  • Users can generate different payment reports such as Form Fill-up, Registration, and Review Payment by selecting the appropriate option from the dropdown.
  • Users can also view payment status reports such as Pending, Success, etc.
  • Users can search for payment details of a specific student using the Registration Number.

Search Result

This section displays the search results based on the criteria selected above.

Export Option: Users can download the complete report in Excel format by clicking the “Export to Excel” button.
